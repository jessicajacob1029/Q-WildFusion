## Interpretation (reported, not fixed)

- **Overfitting after ~epoch 10.** Train box/cls/dfl fall monotonically for all 50 epochs (box 1.89→0.29, cls 0.83→0.12, dfl 1.29→0.80). Val box and dfl losses bottom out at epoch 10 (1.683 / 1.257) and then rise steadily (epoch 50: 1.852 / 1.604). Val mAP50-95 peaks at epoch 10 (0.5784) and ends at 0.5242.
- **Instability on top of that.** Single-epoch collapses at epochs 16 (0.477), 18 (**0.037**; val cls loss 4.27), 23 (0.477), 25 (0.495) and 39 (0.441). Each recovers in the next epoch, and train losses show no corresponding spike, so it is likely a transient in the EMA/BN-statistics path rather than divergence. Cause UNVERIFIED.
- **L_q is optimised hard.** It falls from −0.04 to −0.92 (the variance term dominates, with the ×B scaling of B2). L_rel stays at ~0.002–0.01.
- **B6 works, and training partly undoes k=2 at P5.** The eval gate std is 0.008–0.033 (pre-fix 0.0003–0.0015), so the gate is image-dependent. At P5, mean |γ| grows 1.30 → 1.78 (effective gain 0.65 → 0.89), and eval saturation rises to 20–28 %. At P3/P4, γ shrinks (effective gain 0.23–0.40) and saturation stays ≤5 % (eval).
- **Timing.** Median 1362 s/epoch. Epoch 38 took 6546 s, an outlier (host load or sleep); excluded via the median.

## Caveat for choosing EPOCHS from this curve

- **Selection on the reported split.** The val split is also the reported test split, so picking EPOCHS from the canary's val curve is a (mild) selection on the reported data. Disclose it, or justify the choice from val *loss* (train/val divergence), not val mAP.
- **The epoch-10 value does not predict a 10-epoch run.** With linear LR decay to lrf and 3 warmup epochs, a 10-epoch run anneals much earlier. At epoch 10 of the 50-epoch schedule, the logged LR was still 0.00164 (82 % of the 0.002 AdamW base LR); it reached 6e-5 by epoch 50.
